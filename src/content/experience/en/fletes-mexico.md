## Role and context

At Fletes México I lead the technical architecture of the logistics platform that connects carriers, operators, and clients in the Mexican market. The work combines systems design, technical leadership, and direct implementation in an actively growing organization.

## Core responsibilities

The platform handles complex logistics flows: load assignment, real-time tracking, invoice reconciliation, and operational reporting. My work spans designing the architecture that supports these flows, implementing the most critical modules, and guiding the team through day-to-day technical decisions.

The primary stack is FastAPI with PostgreSQL, deployed with Docker. The Business Intelligence layer uses Power BI connected to the production database to generate the dashboards the operations team relies on in real time.

## Impact

Automating reconciliation workflows reduced by 65% the time the operations team spent on that task manually. The BI dashboards gave leadership real-time decision-making data instead of weekly Excel reports.
