## The problem

Bloomotion, a growing logistics platform, needed a corporate website that communicated its value proposition to B2B prospects: integration capabilities, measurable results, and platform reliability. The site needed to project the visual maturity of an established product and stand out from generic industry sites.

## The solution

I built the site with Flutter Web to maintain technological consistency with the client's team. The site includes animated sections with progressive counters (platform metrics), reveal-on-scroll animations, and a widget-per-section architecture: hero, capabilities, solutions, integrations, testimonials, FAQ, and contact.

**Note on Bloomotion Landing v1**: An earlier version of the site (`bloom`) was a static HTML landing page. This Flutter Web version replaced and expanded that initial work — its content and narrative are fully integrated here.

## My role

I designed the widget architecture, implemented entry animations and progressive counters, and set up Firebase hosting for deployment. I worked directly with the Bloomotion team to refine the technical copywriting in the capabilities and integrations sections.

## Outcome

The corporate site became Bloomotion's primary sales tool in commercial meetings, replacing PowerPoint presentations. The metrics animations ("X shipments processed", "Y% reduction in transit times") gave the sales team concrete conversation starters with prospects.

## Notable learning

Flutter Web for marketing sites involves a clear trade-off: visual fidelity is high but the initial bundle is larger than an equivalent Astro site. For a B2B corporate audience on desktop with stable connections, that's acceptable. For a personal portfolio with a mixed audience, it isn't — hence the choice of Astro for this site.
